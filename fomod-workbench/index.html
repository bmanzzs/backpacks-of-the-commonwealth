How this workbench works ↗

Open an extracted mod folder containing fomod/ModuleConfig.xml. Selecting the package root lets the workbench check file paths and show included images.

Your browser may call this an “upload.” Files are read locally and stay on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server, installed, or copied into your game.

Open XML for quick editing without the package. Source files and local images cannot be verified in this mode.

The wizard previews the FOMOD’s choices and rules. Its appearance is a Workbench design; MO2 and Vortex render their own interfaces.

The XML draft is saved locally when your browser permits. Download XML to save a portable file. Reopen the package folder after reloading to restore images and file checks.
